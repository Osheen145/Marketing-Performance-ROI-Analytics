## 1. Executive Summary

Over the past 12 months, our business deployed **₹6.13 Crore** across 8 marketing channels, generating **₹12.98 Crore** in net attributed revenue and **₹6.85 Crore** in gross profit, delivering a blended portfolio ROAS of **2.12x** and an LTV:CAC ratio of **3.82x**.

However, our standard last-click reporting masks two severe capital allocation hazards:
1. **The Last-Touch Attribution Illusion**: Google Brand Search appears to generate an **8.21x ROAS** with ₹4.03 Crore in revenue. In reality, **74% of these converting customers were originated by top-of-funnel channels (YouTube Video Ads and Meta Prospecting)**. Cutting upper-funnel channels will starve brand search conversions.
2. **Channel Saturation & Bidding Inefficiency**: Branded search query impression share is already at 92%. Pouring incremental capital into brand search results in **CPC inflation** (bidding against ourselves and organic rank) rather than incremental order volume.

**The Answer to the ₹10 Lakh Capital Allocation Question:**
> *"If I give you another ₹10 lakh next month, where should you spend it — and what do you expect to get back?"*

We recommend allocating the ₹10,00,000 across 5 high-yield channels based on marginal ROAS (mROAS) response modeling:
- **₹3,50,000 (35%) $\rightarrow$ Meta Ads Retargeting** (mROAS: 4.20x | Projected Revenue: ₹14.70 Lakh)
- **₹3,00,000 (30%) $\rightarrow$ Affiliate Partner Network** (mROAS: 3.45x | Projected Revenue: ₹10.35 Lakh)
- **₹1,50,000 (15%) $\rightarrow$ LinkedIn B2B Sponsored** (mROAS: 2.80x | Projected Revenue: ₹4.20 Lakh)
- **₹1,50,000 (15%) $\rightarrow$ Meta Ads Prospecting** (mROAS: 2.65x | Projected Revenue: ₹3.98 Lakh)
- **₹50,000 (5%) $\rightarrow$ Email CRM Automations** (mROAS: 5.50x | Projected Revenue: ₹2.75 Lakh)

**Total Financial Return**:

## 3. Unit Economics & Customer Quality Audit

Evaluating customer cohorts across city tiers and segments reveals distinct unit economic behaviors:
1. **Tier 1 Dominance**: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru represent **66.3% of total revenue** with an average AOV of ₹3,840 and a 54.2% gross profit margin.
2. **The "Value Seeker" Trap**: Value seekers make up 40% of acquisition volume on unbranded Google Search, but exhibit a **75% churn rate** after order 1 and consume steep 22% average discounts.
3. **LinkedIn Enterprise Cohorts**: While LinkedIn CAC is high (₹9,800), customers acquired via LinkedIn order at a **₹6,800+ AOV** with an annual repeat purchase rate of 42%, yielding a healthy 3.2x LTV:CAC ratio.

## 4. The 3 Ranked Executive Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Aggressively Scale Middle-Funnel & Performance Affiliates (+₹6.5 Lakh)
- **Action**: Allocate ₹3.5L to Meta Retargeting and ₹3.0L to Affiliate Partners.
- **Why**: These channels operate in the highest elasticity zone. Abandoned-cart audiences on Meta convert with a 4.2x marginal return, and affiliate networks offer performance-based CPA contracts in high-growth Tier-2 markets (Pune, Hyderabad).
- **Projected Impact**: **₹25.05 Lakh in incremental revenue** and **₹6.53 Lakh in net profit**.

### Recommendation 2: Replenish Top-of-Funnel & Expand B2B Buyers (+₹3.0 Lakh)
- **Action**: Allocate ₹1.5L to Meta Prospecting and ₹1.5L to LinkedIn Sponsored Ads.
- **Why**: Retargeting pools deplete without ongoing top-of-funnel replenishment. Meta prospecting lookalikes expand the audience base, while LinkedIn captures premium, high-retention cohorts.
- **Projected Impact**: **₹8.18 Lakh in immediate revenue** + compounding 12-month LTV pipeline.

### Recommendation 3: Freeze Incremental Expansion on Branded Search (₹0 Incremental)
- **Action**: Cap Google Brand Search budget at its baseline ₹12,000/day. Set impression share bidding target to 90% rather than maximizing spend.
- **Why**: Incremental bids on branded queries produce diminishing returns of 1.6x mROAS due to auction inflation and cannibalization of organic search results.
- **Projected Impact**: **Saves ₹1.8 Lakh per quarter in avoidable ad waste**.

## 5. Risk Assessment & Limitations

1. **Attribution Lookback Window**: Our models apply a 30-day window. B2B touchpoints taking >30 days from initial impression to closed order may show understated first-touch credit.
2. **Privacy & Signal Loss**: iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and third-party cookie phase-out restrict client-side cross-domain tracking. We recommend deploying Server-Side Conversions API (CAPI) to preserve signal fidelity.
3. **Capacity Constraints**: Email CRM marginal return (5.5x) cannot be scaled indefinitely; it is strictly constrained by active opt-in database size.
